Our story

A singers' circle on the Godavari

We are a newly formed organisation of singers based in Rajahmundry. Our aim is simple: to help people sing well and to keep Telugu music in everyday life.

Swara Raga Sudha began as a small group of singers who wanted regular practice, honest feedback and chances to perform. We teach in Telugu and English, with a focus on the works of Tyagaraja, Annamayya and Bhadrachala Ramadasu, along with devotional and light music.

Objectives లక్ష్యాలు

What we set out to do

  • Teach singing with proper foundations and low fees.
  • Present concerts and give new singers a stage.
  • Preserve and share Telugu keertanas and compositions.
  • Support students who cannot afford training.
  • Build a supportive community of singers in the Godavari region.

    Practice సాధన

    Practise with us

    Two simple tools for daily practice. Sound plays only after you press a button.

    Tambura shruti

    A four-string tambura: Pa (or Ma), Sa, Sa, lower Sa. Pick your kattai and sing along.

    Adi Talam

    Eight beats: a laghu of four (clap, little, ring and middle fingers), then two drutams (clap and wave each).
