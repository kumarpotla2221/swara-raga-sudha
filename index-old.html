About Swara Raga Sudha

We are a newly formed organisation of singers based in Rajahmundry, on the banks of the Godavari. Our aim is simple: to help people sing well and to keep Telugu music in everyday life.

Swara Raga Sudha began recently as a small group of singers who wanted regular practice, honest feedback and chances to perform. We are growing step by step and welcome people who want to help build the organisation from the start.

We teach in Telugu and English, with a focus on the works of Tyagaraja, Annamayya and Bhadrachala Ramadasu, along with devotional and light music.

Our objectives

  • Teach singing with proper foundations and low fees.
  • Present concerts and give new singers a stage.
  • Preserve and share Telugu keertanas and compositions.
  • Support students who cannot afford training.
  • Build a supportive community of singers in the Godavari region.

సాధన

Practise with us

Two simple tools for daily practice. Sound plays only after you press a button.

Tambura shruti

A four-string tambura: Pa (or Ma), Sa, Sa, lower Sa. Pick your kattai and sing along.

Adi Talam

Eight beats: a laghu of four (clap, little, ring and middle fingers), then two drutams (clap and wave each).
